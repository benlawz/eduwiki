# Agency Hub

Welcome to the Agency Hub—your central resource for understanding and developing agency in learning and life.

## What is Agency?

Agency is the capacity to take initiative, make independent choices, and drive projects forward rather than simply taking orders. In an AI-enhanced world, agency becomes the crucial differentiator between those who design the future and those who follow instructions.

## Core Concepts

- **[[Agency]]** — The ability to choose, reflect, and shape one's own trajectory
- **Autonomy** — The freedom to make decisions and act independently
- **Self-direction** — Taking responsibility for one's own learning and growth
- **Initiative** — The ability to identify opportunities and act on them

## Practical Applications

### For Educators
- Create opportunities for student choice and voice
- Provide constructive feedback that focuses on effort and strategy
- Model agency in your own learning and decision-making
- Design learning experiences that require initiative and problem-solving

### For Learners
- Start small with minor projects or process improvements
- Define your own goals rather than waiting for direction
- Take initiative on problems you identify
- Practice making decisions and reflecting on outcomes

### For Parents
- Encourage autonomy with age-appropriate choices and responsibilities
- Foster growth mindset by valuing effort over innate ability
- Support child-led, project-based learning based on interests
- Model agency in your own life and decision-making

## Try This Now

Choose one small area of your life where you can practice agency:

1. **Identify a problem** you can solve or improve
2. **Define your own goal** for addressing it
3. **Take the first step** without waiting for permission
4. **Reflect on what you learned** from the experience

Remember: Agency is like a muscle—it gets stronger with practice.
