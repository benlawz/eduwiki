# Knowledge

Knowledge is the theoretical or practical understanding of a subject. It encompasses facts, information, descriptions, or skills acquired through experience or education.

## Types of Knowledge

1. **Declarative Knowledge** (Know-What)
   - Facts and concepts
   - Theoretical understanding
   - Information that can be declared or stated

2. **Procedural Knowledge** (Know-How)
   - Skills and procedures
   - Practical abilities
   - How to perform specific tasks

3. **Meta-Knowledge** (Know-About)
   - Understanding of one's own knowledge
   - Learning how to learn
   - Self-awareness in learning

## Knowledge Acquisition

> [!note] Learning Pathways
> There are multiple ways to acquire knowledge:
> - Direct experience
> - Formal education
> - Self-directed learning
> - Social interaction
