## When to use this
You’re overwhelmed, stuck, or unsure where to begin.

## Steps
1. **Pick one valuable outcome** for the next 7 days.
2. List **3 atomic actions** you can do in under 15 minutes.
3. Block **two 25-minute sprints** on your calendar for those actions.
4. Complete one atomic action during your first sprint.

## Variations
- **Light**: 1 sprint in the week.
- **Medium**: 2–3 sprints.
- **Heavy**: Daily sprints.

## Measure success
- Did you complete 2+ sprints?
- Did your self-rated agency score improve by at least 1 point (0–10 scale)?

> [!tip] Try This Now
> Write your 7–day outcome in one sentence and schedule your first sprint for tomorrow morning.
