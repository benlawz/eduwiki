## Summary  
Cultivate your ability to effect change actively rather than reacting passively. Human agency—the courage to do something new without being told—will separate the designers of the future from the assembly-line workers of the past, and the empowered learner from the spoon-fed student.

---

## Agency in the AI Era  
Human **agency** is the capacity to take initiative, make independent choices, and drive projects forward rather than simply taking orders. With AI and robots poised to automate most work tasks—often more efficiently than humans—agency becomes the crucial differentiator. It’s about acting, not being acted upon, by setting goals and executing them with the tools (like AI) at hand.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

---

## AI Is Changing Roles, Not Just Tools  
AI is *flattening* team structures: product design teams will shrink, with fewer layers of management, because AI makes each team member vastly more productive. Climbing the traditional corporate ladder will become less viable; instead, individuals must act as self-driven leaders within their areas, rather than waiting for direction.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

---

## Why Personal Agency Matters More Than Ever  
- **Proactivity over passivity.** Individuals who identify problems and act will outpace those waiting for instructions.  
- **Judgment and persuasion hinge on agency.** AI can generate many options—but someone still needs to choose wisely and convince others. Human agency is the spark that makes those higher-level skills possible.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

- **Agency is learnable.** Start small—leading a minor project or optimizing a process—and build up your confidence and capacity. The AI-driven workplace rewards those who pair initiative with the ability to decide, not just follow.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

---

## Cultivating Agency in Children  
Building agency early is vital. Traditional education often emphasizes rote learning, which stunts initiative. But when students choose their learning paths, they become more motivated and self-directed—learning how to learn becomes more important than any specific subject.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

Parents can support this by:
- Encouraging autonomy with choice and responsibility.
- Fostering a [[growth-mindset]] and [[internal-locus-of-control]] by valuing effort and [[strategy]] over innate ability.
- Supporting child-led, [[project-based-learning]] based on their interests.  
 (UX Tigers, 2024)

## Try This Now  
Choose a project or task—big or small—that interests you. Then:
1. Define the goal yourself.
2. Pick the first 3 steps you can take immediately.
3. Act on them, and reflect on what you learned afterward.

**Agency isn’t about waiting. It’s about choosing, doing, and growing—especially when AI is the background engine, not the driver.**
