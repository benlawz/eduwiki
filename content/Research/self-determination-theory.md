**Why it matters**  
Agency grows when people feel autonomy, competence, and relatedness — the three pillars of Self-Determination Theory (SDT).

**Key findings:**
- **Autonomy**: Feeling ownership over decisions increases engagement.
- **Competence**: Regularly succeeding at challenges builds confidence.
- **Relatedness**: Strong connections support persistence and resilience.

**Implications:**
- Design learning environments that give choice, progressive challenge, and community.
- Use quick wins to build momentum without overwhelming the learner.
